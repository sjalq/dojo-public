# Kyle Doops — Risk Strategy (synthesized across a full BTC cycle, 2022-04 → 2026-06)

Built from 7 cycle-phase extractions (P1 bear descent · P2 FTX capitulation · P3 2023
accumulation · P4 2024 breakout/halving · P5 2024 mid-cycle chop · P6 2024-25 bull-to-peak ·
P7 2025-26 decline). ~55 host-verified Kyle-solo videos. This is the "between the lines"
through-line: what he actually DOES with risk, regardless of his directional call.

Tenure note: Kyle's first Crypto Banter video is 2022-04-22 (initially standing in for
Sheldon), regular daily host by mid-2022. There is no earlier content to mine.

## The spine: he doesn't predict, he waits for confirmation

- **"No trigger, no trade."** Ironclad entry discipline in every phase. He states a hard
  invalidation BEFORE entering, every time. No setup without a defined level that proves it wrong.
- **Macro trend state = the 21-week EMA.** Above = bull regime, below = bear regime. He will
  not flip his core bias until this (plus his checklist) confirms — even when price tempts him.
- **Symmetric, multi-condition regime calls.** He refuses to call tops or bottoms on price alone:
  - *Bottom criteria (3+):* high-volume capitulation candle + sustained negative funding +
    alts taking out lows / momentum divergence (later: "bull moon"). All must converge.
  - *Top criteria (12-metric dashboard, built iteratively through 2024-25):* PI Cycle Top,
    Puell, MVRV-Z, NUPL, BTC.D, USDT.D, funding, Fear/Greed, exchange volume, even YouTube
    view counts, 4yr-MA ratio. Exits only when these converge at 90-100%.

## Position management (consistent across all 7 phases)

1. **Cash is a position — his default in uncertainty.** Repeatedly fully in cash for months
   (May-Aug 2023; one-daily-close flip to 100% cash Feb 2025; capitulation phases). "Sitting on
   my hands." "No-trade zone." He'd rather miss the first leg than bleed in chop.
2. **Only trade the extremities.** Longs at range low, shorts at range high, nothing in the
   middle. Explicit anti-overtrading rule.
3. **Scale in, never full clip.** 3-5 staged orders; small first tranche (~10%); bigger size
   nearer key supports ("pyramid into levels"). DCA grid bots for accumulation at the lows
   (e.g. entered BTC via grid at ~$19.7k in 2022).
4. **Spot vs leverage split.** Core longs = spot (or 1.5-2x treated as pseudo-spot: "take profit
   without giving up the upside"). Shorts = 3-5x **isolated** max. In volatility he cuts leverage
   hard: "if you use 10x, go to 3x in capitulation." Leverage positions closed when funding cost
   exceeds the opportunity.
5. **Stop-to-breakeven = "free trade" / "eliminate the risk."** Once price moves in his favor he
   moves the stop to break-even. BUT he refuses to do this while sitting ON support ("you'd be
   selling support") — only after a real move.
6. **Partial profit-taking into strength.** 25-75% off at first target. Sell orders framed as
   "insurance, not bank." Trailing stops raised (weekly) only under confirmed daily higher lows.
   Rotates realized profit (e.g. into majors).
7. **Spot core is never sold on drawdown** — only on his exit signals. He firewalls the long-term
   spot bag from tactical leverage trades.
8. **Counter-trend trades are labelled as traps.** He'll take the relief-rally long but calls it
   a bull trap and runs it as a quick, tightly-stopped scoped trade — never as a new trend.
9. **Risk-of-ruin minimization via prop capital.** Used BitFunded/prop accounts to trade chop so
   personal capital isn't the thing at risk.
10. **"Investor → active trader" mode-switch near the top.** As the cycle matured (May 2025) he
    consciously shifted from accumulation/holding to active trading + shorts-as-insurance.

## One-line characterization

A disciplined, trigger-driven swing trader who treats **capital preservation as the edge**:
he sizes small and scales, keeps a spot core firewalled from leverage, sits in cash without
apology, and only commits when his multi-condition checklist (anchored on the 21-week EMA)
converges — calling neither tops nor bottoms on price alone.
