## 2. Analytical Method

**Multi-timeframe structure analysis (HTF bias first, LTF for entry):**

- **21 EMA (weekly)**: Needs multiple candle closes above to confirm bull; below confirms bear. Referenced nearly every video as THE trend-state indicator.
- **9 and 18 EMA cluster** ("pink line"): Short-term trend signal; price must reclaim to shift momentum.
- **200 EMA / 200 MA**: Key swing support/resistance on daily and weekly. Treated as near-mandatory bounce zone.
- **Gaussian Channel**: Red = downtrend; reclaim = bull run activation. Referenced as a high-conviction signal.
- **Super Guppy** (composite EMA indicator): Used for quick color-coded trend reads across multiple assets.

**Price structure:**
- Bearish/bullish order blocks, SR flips (support-resistance flip), swing failure patterns (SFP), inverse fair value gaps (IFV gaps).
- Bear flags, parallel channels, ascending/descending triangles, cup and handle.
- Bearish/bullish engulfing candles ("outside bar candle"), tweezer bottoms.
- Monday range trading (deviation of Monday high/low as entry trigger).

**Fibonacci:**
- Golden pocket (0.618-0.65 zone) is the primary fib tool.
- 0.382, 50%, and 1.618 extensions used for targets.
- "Trend-based fib" for extension targets.

**Momentum/oscillators:**
- **TD Sequential**: 7-8-9 count top/bottom signals. "Nine top" = caution, "nine buy" = long opportunity.
- **RSI**: Uses RSI pivot level (specific horizontal) as a tracking line. Oversold < 20 on weekly = structural interest.
- **MACD**: References cross of zero line as timing signal for bear market duration.

**Volume:**
- **Daily exchange volume with 7-day MA** is his primary market health signal. Declining = no sustainable rally. He checks this nearly every video.
- CME futures chart vs retail chart: uses CME to filter false weekend signals.

**Market structure/flow:**
- **USDT dominance**: Rising = bearish Bitcoin. Targets breakout above 9-9.3% weekly close as key signal.
- **DXY**: Rising = risk-off, applied pressure on crypto. Tracks multi-decade trend line and breakout level.
- **10-year yield**: Used to anticipate Fed pressure and equity risk.
- **Funding rates** (aggregate): Positive = long-crowded risk. Negative = short-squeeze potential.
- **Open interest**: Uses Velo Charts. Rising OI with falling spot = potential trap.
- **Liquidity heat maps**: Tracks where stops are stacked (24-hour, weekly view). Called out in dollar amounts.
- **ETF flows**: Weekly inflows/outflows as sentiment extremes for BTC.
- **Coinbase premium index**: Green at local tops = warning.
- **Fear & Greed Index**: Used as secondary sentiment check.
- **Bear/bull moons**: References lunar timing cycles with ~80% hit rate claimed.
- **Wyckoff**: Distribution and reaccumulation schematics, markup/markdown phases referenced by name.
- **4-year Bitcoin cycle**: Used as duration/timing map. Cycle bottoms at ~360-365 days from peak.
- **Price cycle fractals**: Overlays current cycle against 2014-15, 2018-19, 2021-22 bear markets.
- **Volume points of control (VPOC)**: Used on individual setups.
- **Anchored VWAP**: Used as dynamic support/resistance from key swing points.
- **Bollinger Band width percentile**: Contraction → expansion signal.

## 3. Risk Management Tells

This is the core of his content. Every video contains explicit risk management instruction.

**Position sizing and scaling:**
- Never enters full position at once. Always scales in incrementally ("pyramid approach", "first trade, second trade, third trade").
- Holds 10-15% initial allocation, builds as levels confirm.
- Takes 25-50% profits at first target before trailing the rest. Often 75% off at initial TP.
- "Level to level" profit-taking: locks in each level before opening the next.

**Stop management:**
- Stop at break even = "free trade" / "the trade is safe" / "eliminate the risk" / "can't lose on the trade now".
- Explicit invalidation levels stated before every trade. "As soon as you break below X, it invalidates on the long side."
- Trails stops under confirmed higher lows.
- Raises stops to recent swing support as trade progresses.

**Entry discipline:**
- "No trigger, no trade" — refuses entries without a low-time-frame confirmation signal.
- Specific confirmation patterns required: reclaim of a key level, reaction out of a demand zone, bullish structure candle.
- "I would wait. I would wait, wait, wait."
- Does not chase pumps in bear market ("don't chase the pumps — short the pumps after a couple of days").

**Cash as a position:**
- Explicitly holds cash ("majority in cash for the majority of this year") and endorses it as a valid trade.
- "I'd rather be bored but not losing money."
- Differentiates between long-term investment and short-term trading explicitly: "there's a big difference."
- "Sitting in cash" = endorsed waiting strategy.

**Leverage stance:**
- Preference for low-leverage isolated positions.
- Mentions 2x-3x long as acceptable with isolated margin, not cross margin.
- Explicitly warns against over-leverage: "if you can't deal with that, unfortunately you might have to do something else."
- Treats prop firm challenges (BitFunded, Blofin Beat the Bear) as a way for viewers to access capital without risking own funds.

**Conviction and probability framing:**
- Always frames calls as probabilities, not certainties ("high probability", "most likely", "there is a real possibility").
- States both bull and bear cases explicitly ("the case for both").
- "Difference between possibility and probability — those are two very different things."
- Frequently reminds: "history doesn't always repeat one-for-one, but it often rhymes."

**Altcoin-specific risk:**
- "99.9% of coins go to zero against Bitcoin" — stated as empirical fact.
- Never swing-trades or position-trades altcoins; only short-term trades with defined stops.
- Instructs viewers to check altcoin charts priced in BTC on CoinGecko for historical context.

**Not-financial-advice hedging:**
- Rarely says "not financial advice" explicitly; instead uses "each to their own", "you have to do your own research", and "I'm not saying you should do X" as softer disclaimers.
