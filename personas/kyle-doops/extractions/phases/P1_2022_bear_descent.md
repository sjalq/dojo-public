## 2. Market Read This Phase

**Overall bias:** Strongly, consistently bearish. Never flipped constructive until conditions were explicitly met.

**Price targets named (level-to-level approach):**
- April 2022: Needs $43k reclaim to turn bullish. Support $39k, next $34–35k, then $29k if lost.
- May 2022: $21.4k (monthly 50 MA), $20k "100% likely to lose over time," $34–35k liquidity box.
- June–July 2022: $19k critical, $13,880 key yearly level (quarterly low wick), $10,760 next, $10–12k ultimate buy zone.
- August 2022: Relief rally stalled at $24–25k. Resistance $27–28k. Downside range lows $19,200 / $18k.
- September 2022: Inverter chart ascending triangle measured move to $8k BTC (inverter). Upright targets: $16,153 next, $13,880, then $10–12k liquidity.
- October 2022: Descending triangle on 4H with $10–12k measured move. Stops at $17,592 wick low as the decisive invalidation.

**Bottom-calling stance:** Refused to call a bottom throughout. Used three explicit criteria: (1) high-volume capitulation candle, (2) negative funding rates held for extended period, (3) price reclaims key moving averages with confirmation. None of these conditions were met during P1 in his assessment.

## 3. Risk Posture

**Cash as position:** "Pretty much just sitting on my hands." "For those of you that have been cash heavy as I've recommended for many months now you're going to be in a phenomenal position." Explicitly endorsed sitting out the entire move: "rather wait for an established uptrend where the market is going to be poised in a much safer position."

**Leverage stance:** "Trading spot at the moment is probably your best bet, I wouldn't be trading on Leverage." For the few shorts he took, maximum 3–5x isolated. "I'm building a short position" (August 2022) at $23–24k. Strongly warned against leverage longs: "when Bitcoin is in such a major indecision point...altcoins are going to be very tough to buy."

**Sizing:** 3–5 orders total; first entry 10% of allocation; builds towards invalidation ("I leave the final 50% for the bottom as I'm about to get invalidated"). Never enters full position at once.

**Stop management / invalidations:** Explicit invalidation level stated before every trade. April: "As soon as you start closing candles below the moving average, it gets a little bit airy." June: invalidation at $19,616 (wicking below previous range low). Always moves stop to break even once first TP hit.

**Entry discipline:** "No trigger no trade" implicit throughout. Waited for: "negative funding rates held negative for quite some time," "high volume capitulation," "reclaim of key moving average and it's angled upwards." In practice, he entered zero spot longs in this phase on main account — only small shorts on bounces.

**Profit-taking:** 50% at TP1, trail rest. On short trades: first 50% closed at first support zone, second 50% held for continuation.

**Altcoin stance:** Explicitly told viewers to check altcoin charts priced in BTC on CoinGecko. Avoided swing-long altcoins: "your primary liquidity zone coming all the way at the bottom... still another 56% drop" (MATIC). Only range-traded altcoins in Oct 2022 as stagnant-market strategy.
