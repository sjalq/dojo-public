## 2. Market Read

### How he called (or missed) the cycle top

Kyle did not issue a single hard "top is in" declaration at the Nov 2025 ATH. He layered in a sequence of escalating warnings:

**Sep 16, 2025:** Weekly trend still intact. Daily showing "change of character" (new swing low). He flagged the Aug 14 down-candle as the key resistance to watch for a rejection hedge: "This is where you want to watch out for any kind of a rejection. It makes sense to take a hedge trade." Targeting 120K as the natural rejection zone, but still holding long bias on weekly.

**Oct 11, 2025:** A $19.3B liquidation flash-crash (described as "bigger than COVID"). BTC was near ATH territory (weekly close level he gave as the key test: $111,597). He used the cycle timing argument: "1,067 days into the cycle... timing-wise, we've got to that point where we're pretty close to the end." He identified the critical risk signal pre-crash: "ETF inflows were at record highs — anytime we see this, we know we're very close to a local top." He didn't write off the cycle yet ("risk metrics relatively muted, not in the red zone"), but warned the next move to ~$120K would be a "bearish retest with the opportunity to exit."

**Dec 16-17, 2025:** Explicit capitulation framing. Bear flag on BTC (3 days of opens and closes below key levels). BTC at ~$86K, tight range, targeting 69-72K. 4/7 Magnificent Seven stocks showing weakness, "maybe Bitcoin was leading that weakness." MSTR: "Adam and Eve pattern is the canary in the coal mine." Short entered at the ATH top, "up over 1,000% on that trade." Calling time-based vs. price-based capitulation openly.

**Jan 27 – Feb 2, 2026 (triage):** "3 Reasons Why I Wouldn't Buy Bitcoin Yet" (Jan 27) followed by "Bitcoin Breakdown Has Just Begun" (Feb 2) — the Gaussian channel lost, 21 EMA confirmed bear. This is his public flip-to-bear declaration.

## 3. RISK POSTURE (most important)

### De-risking cadence

Kyle's risk posture in P7 was a textbook progression from hedge → scale out → full short → cash:

**Sep-Oct 2025:** Still long on weekly, but hedging at key rejection zones. "Insurance stop goes slightly above; you can just see how things may play out... take out all those short traders... then reject."

**Dec 2025:** Full de-risk by mid-December. 4/7 Mag7 stocks weak. MSTR short running 1,000%+ (75% already taken off). Cash accumulation advised. "Volume continues to diminish... it's pretty much impossible to have any sustainable rally."

**Jan-Feb 2026 (triage):** "Wouldn't Buy Bitcoin Yet" — not participating in any longs. "Majority in cash."

**Counter-trend longs in bear market (trap framing):** Kyle was consistent that recoveries are not buyable until criteria are met. In March 2026, squeezes to 80-85K were framed as: "I simply just treat this as a dead cat bounce." He noted "I'm personally not looking to participate in these moves — it's a counter-trend trade." The mechanism he cited: "You got your stop and reversal indicator at $80,000, and your 18 EMA at $83,000. You look to fade that."

**The "no trigger no trade" discipline (Jun 2026):** After failing to bounce at 61,800 in mid-June: "There is no trigger, no trigger on the trade. Do not take the trade over here because there's no reclaim." He explicitly called out the longs building on the way down: "funding rate is going positive as price is coming down... you could get more wrecked long traders."

**Cash as the dominant posture:** "I'd rather be bored but not losing money." Volume was his gating condition: "The rallies won't be sustainable without that daily exchange volume, which is still declining."

**Leverage and stops:**
- MSTR short: 75% closed at first target; 25% left running (stops at break even = "free trade")
- BTC short by June 10: "up almost $40,000." Stop-management model applied — trailing under confirmed lower highs.
- Counter-trend long June 26 (60K bounce): 10x cross-margin, risking $1K, TPs at 63K, 66K, 69K, 4.3:1 R:R. Explicit framing: "I'm not taking this because I think the dump is over — just because risk-to-reward makes sense."

### Capitulation re-run criteria (Jun 2026)

Kyle applied the same bottom-checklist framework from prior cycles:

Criteria triggered by Jun 26:
- Fear & Greed: 13% (anger/panic territory)
- USDT dominance: weekly close above 9.3% (breakout)
- DXY: confirmed daily candle close above breakout level
- Sentiment: "anger phase — blame Saylor"
- Longs getting wiped back-to-back ($1B/day)

Criteria still not met by Jun 26:
- "Full-blown sickness" bottom — not yet declared
- Volume spike capitulation at support
- Momentum divergence (Market Cipher B green dot)
- Bull moon catalyst (expected Tue July 1)
- "I do not want time-based capitulation — I want the band-aid ripped off"

His stated preference: "I'd prefer a price-based capitulation within the next couple of weeks, possibly in the first two weeks of July."
