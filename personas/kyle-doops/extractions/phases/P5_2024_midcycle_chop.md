## 3. Risk Posture

**Primary rule: range extremities only.**
"look to buy support and sell resistance" — applies throughout every video in this phase. The chop is not traded; the extremes are.

**Entry discipline:**
- "best buys are always going to be as close to the range low as possible especially if you sweep that zone" (XbI7EfFGiJ8)
- Required trigger for conservative entry: "wait for price to push back above — wait for the higher low — that is your next best zone with greater confluence so you're not catching a falling knife" (voNC4i09gW4)
- Aggressive traders: enter at the level with stop below. Conservative: wait for higher high + higher low confirmation before re-entering.

**No-trade zone in chop:**
"conservative Traders would consider this whole area a no trade zone until such time as we breach the downtrend" (sGaGfhVDbos) — explicitly endorsing sitting out the mid-range during choppy conditions.

**Pyramid into levels — never all-in at once:**
"you want to stagger your orders into the market... buy a little bit over there, a little bit more over here, even more over here — as you get closer and closer to the key levels you'll buy bigger and bigger portions into those zones" (kqdBnZAtQiw)

**Stop management:**
"I raise my stops above my entry — I would actually stop out in slight profit and I'd rather attempt to reenter trying to get the perfect trade" (BSEgD-MNte8). Stop at breakeven = "free trade", held live leverage positions throughout but ran tight invalidations.

**Sizing in chop:**
Used BitFunded (prop firm) accounts to trade the chop — framing it as a way to risk only the challenge fee ($49) rather than personal capital. Effectively endorsed trading chop with house money.

**Invalidation levels stated every video:**
- Below $60k monthly close = structural damage
- Below $66,667 = "danger zone" opens path to $44k
- Below $44k = possible end of bull structure
- Above $65k daily close = trend shifts to bull, re-enter fully

**Patience framing:**
"all you have to do is you need to be patient right — wait for a shift in the market, wait for a break of structure" (kqdBnZAtQiw)
"risk returns very very quickly back into the markets when it does — so you probably want to have your positions established" (3u0_LwcTkMI)

**Altcoin risk during chop:**
Explicitly said most altcoins will range 3-6 months. Only accumulate the selectively strongest (Casper, Sui, Mantra cited repeatedly). "You're BURNING Cash If You Buy Altcoins Now" was the surrounding message during deep chop.
