## 2. Market Read

**Phase arc:** Kyle tracked BTC from ~$45k (pre-ETF) through $73k ATH (Mar 2024) and back into post-halving consolidation around $60-63k. His cycle framing used four phases keyed to the monthly RSI:
- Phase 3 = RSI crosses 73 (Mar 2024 call on VFaHyX1SDbg)
- Phase 4 = RSI crosses 80 / true parabolic (Gv6te6QINuQ, Mar 11 at $70k)

**Bias:** Macro-bullish throughout but explicitly watched for local tops at every resistance cluster.

**Key level calls:**
- $48k: 1000-BTC sell wall flagged Jan 9. Tagged to "the exact tick" Jan 10. Resistance.
- $50k-$52k: "Major resistance zone" through Jan-Feb. Set personal sell orders there.
- $59k: Post-ATH, the floor that "must not give multiple weekly closes below."
- $60k: "Personally I think I'm going to be selling at $60,000 especially if I see the heat maps showing bright yellow... it's been too quick."
- $67,500: "Must must must get above" to confirm inverse H&S (Apr 17 pre-halving call).
- $74k: Measured move from inverse H&S (Mar 22 call).
- $48k (again): Post-halving, "aggressive buy" confluence zone = high-time-frame mid-range + 200-day MA.
- $52k: Leveraged traders' worst-case floor; bids expected there.

**Top-watching:** Used TD Sequential count, RSI divergence, heat map sell-order density on Trading Light, funding rate frothiness, fear and greed above 90, and Coinbase Premium signals. Never called the final top — only managed exposure.

## 3. Risk Posture (Most Important)

### Pre-ATH (Jan-Feb 2024) — Riding the Breakout
- Held original spot entry since **$17,100 with zero selling** throughout the entire $17k → $73k move.
- On the ETF false-approval day (Jan 10): "I personally will be looking to take some crypto off the table should we get a move any higher into this 50k region."
- Placed a **sell order at $48-50k zone** as a partial exit; also constructed hedge shorts on weak altcoins as "insurance."
- Entry structure: pyramid of **5 orders** — "if I'm wrong at least three times and it comes lower, well I've got 60% of my orders in." First entry just 20% of total position size.
- Apex trade example: "We took 75% of our profit off... left some on the table just to participate without any form of FOMO."
- Low leverage treated as pseudo-spot: "$150,000 Casper position at 3x, think of it more as a spot trade."

### ATH Phase (Mar 2024) — Managing Euphoria
- Phase 4 parabolic declared but **no mass exits**. Rationale: "80% of all the returns that you're ever making in crypto will be made in that final 20% of the cycle" — exiting too early destroys the bulk of gains.
- Fear and greed at 82 (not extreme enough to trigger exit): "I would expect this would be a whole lot higher once you start to break those all-time highs."
- Meme coin profits being **actively rotated** into longer-term alts (Casper, Injective, Solana) rather than held.
- Explicit meme coin profit-taking: "Take profit take profit be careful, don't get wrecked over there."
- Near trade at 3000%+ ($17,400-$20,000 in profit) — held open with note: "if it sets in a lower high I might have to consider closing the leverage position."
- Stop tracking: "track underneath each higher low" as the trailing stop mechanism.

### Pre-Halving Consolidation (Apr 17, 2024)
- "This is not the environment to take a ton of trades right now."
- "Being in a cash position can also be a trade while you wait for the next major move."
- "Only trade at the extremities of the range — shorts at the Range High, longs at the range low, and nothing else in between."
- Spot holders: "sit there, be patient, ride out the storm — for you it means nothing."
- Leveraged holders: "you need to be able to withstand a Bitcoin sell-off down to $52,000. Ask yourself what that would mean for your respective altcoin."
- Whale Room: no new trades opened during chop. "We're looking for opportunities to build longer-term positions into strong altcoins that we can hold for the rest of the cycle."
- Closed / reduced leverage if funding became expensive: "the funding rates will end up eating me alive, I'll rather reload."

### Post-Halving (Apr 25, 2024)
- BTC rejected off 50-day MA. Monitoring for transition from range to downtrend.
- "My system hasn't said go short yet. It said we're transitioning from a strong trending market into now a slow trending market and moving into a range."
- $59k = weekly hold line. Below it with multiple closes = serious warning; mid-range at $48k would be "aggressive buy."
- "I made quite substantial gains over the last couple of years by not fading the trend. I will let you know when my system says go short."
- "Have enough capital to deploy all the way down to about $48,000 — still fair game for buy the dip opportunity."
- Funding rates went negative post-halving: "negative funding in a bull run is a screaming buy opportunity... you don't release your entire clip in one go, you look to slowly allocate and DCA into longer-term positions."
- Flagged "sell in May": "hedge fund managers around the world tend to go on holiday in the US around their summer break in May, be aware of that."
