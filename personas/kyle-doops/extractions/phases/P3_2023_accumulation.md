## 2. Market Read

**Overall bias**: Cautious bull with active bear-market-relief-rally framing throughout. Kyle never capitulated to full bearishness but consistently labelled bounces as "relief rallies" until at least May. He confirmed a bullish market structure shift only gradually, primarily anchored to the 4-year halving cycle clock.

**Phase-by-phase read:**

- **Feb 2023 (BTC ~$24-25k)**: "Yes Bitcoin can go to 10 to 12K still — that was one of my main targets — but right now the higher probability trade is looking to bid the higher lows." Treated $27k as the next target if the ascending triangle broke. Saw the MACD crossing above zero on weekly as key confirmation. Still outright possible to go lower.

- **Mar 2023 (banking crisis, BTC $20k → $28k)**: Strong conviction that the bear market was transitioning. "When there's terrible news but prices continue to go up — it's a bull market." But stressed the move up was potentially too fast. His explicit forecast: if BTC "rapidly gets to $28k in the matter of a couple of days" that is a fade, not a hold. Watching $28k / $32k as exit levels.

- **Apr 2023 (BTC rejection from ~$30k, back to $26k)**: More cautious again. Monthly chart printed a dragonfly doji on low volume — bearish signal. Watching $25k as the critical level; breach = back to the long bear range targeting $18k.

- **May 2023 (BTC range $25-28k)**: Revealed he had already sold. "One year to the halving" clock confirmed he is long-term bullish but short-term in cash. "I think it is a transitionary phase and we're slowly moving into a bull market but there is a lot going on with traditional markets."

- **Aug 2023 (BTC loses $25k)**: First unambiguously bearish short-term read of the phase. "Losing the 21 EMA and 200 MA on the weekly is pretty significant." Running short trades. "Bitcoin is range bound and expected to continue to be so — for probably the rest of this year."

- **Oct 2023 (BTC ~$26-28k, 200d/200w rejection)**: Balanced re-entry narrative. October statistically bullish (82% of Octobers green in BTC history). Watching 200-day MA slope — still angled up unlike 2021 death cross. ETF delay priced in; timing aligns with halving run into early 2024.
