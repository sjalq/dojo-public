## 2. His Market Read This Phase

**Overall stance: Bearish to cautiously accumulating, never bottom-calling until confirmed.**

Throughout Nov 2022 Kyle maintained that no bottom was in. His framework was explicit:

- **Three capitulation criteria** (all unmet on 2022-11-10): (1) extremely negative aggregate funding rates, (2) high-volume spike on aggregate exchanges, (3) altcoins taking out all-time lows. All three had to fire simultaneously.
- **2018 fractal** (beUL9ZBOSLo, 2022-11-11): He mapped the $18K base to the 2018 $6K base. In 2018, price wicked to $5,248, bounced to ~$5,700, then dropped 50% to $3,200 final low. Analogy: $18K → bounce → $11K or $10K.
- **Cycle timing** (7ns4J-DgVyA, 2022-11-24): 54 weeks from ATH to current bottom — within the 52–59 week historical range — making him say they were "close timing-wise" but price still had further to go.

**Levels named:**

| Level | Context |
|-------|---------|
| $21,500 | 21-week EMA — must reclaim to confirm trend reversal (every video) |
| $19,000 / $18,900 | Range reclaim = flip bullish (tactical level, Nov 10–11) |
| $18,150 | June 2022 low — first major resistance / take-profit zone on Dec bounce |
| $17,500 | Key 2017 level / first de-risk trigger on Dec long |
| $16,670 | Hard stop on Dec long — below here = out |
| $13,880 | Key quarterly/yearly level — "at the very least expect a tag of that" |
| $12,000 | First horizontal buy zone |
| $10,000–$11,500 | Ultimate sweet spot / mid-range of major range; "absolute ape-in" zone |
| $8,700 | Worst-case scenario — long-term 2017 trend line |

**Was the bottom called?** No. He explicitly said on 2022-11-24: "I don't believe that Bitcoin or crypto is dead uh and I don't think that the bottom is in yet either." The cycle ultimately bottomed at ~$15.5K in Nov 2022 before Kyle shifted to Dec relief-rally mode — he never identified $15.5K as the macro bottom in this phase.
