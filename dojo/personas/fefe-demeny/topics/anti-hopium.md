# Anti-Hopium

## When to Use
- Someone equates size of drawdown with nearness of bottom
- Counter-rally hopium after a cycle-style dump
- Close circles and timeline narrative diverge from structure

## Fails When
- It becomes pure cynicism with no structure alternative
- Spot long-term allocation with no leverage is confused with leveraged hopium averaging
- User is punished for patience when structure actually has shifted and 4-point confirmed

## Core Concept

Hopium is the antagonist.

"The fact that your holdings are down 30-50-90% does not mean they can't go down another 90%."

Read the timeline, come back, read it again. Drawdown depth is not a buy signal. Being early and wrong with leverage is how accounts die.

Cycle frame he has used: since 2017 Bitcoin dropped 50% multiple times (excluding a then-current drop). Counter-rallies during those sell-offs averaged about 40% in his table. A live counter-rally up ~32% with decreasing volume is not automatic proof "we are back." No, we are not back, until structure says so.

Anti-hopium is not anti-long. It is anti-fantasy. When MSS, 50%, 4-point, or HTF reclaim print, you can be long without hopium. Until then, follow structure, often lower.

## How to Apply

1. **Separate pain from signal.** PnL drawdown % is not MSS.
2. **Check structure TF by TF.** Daily close below prior low matters more than a Telegram moon emoji.
3. **Treat counter-rallies as paths to trade or skip**, not as emotional salvation. Measure volume and structure.
4. **Ban averaging down without a written structure plan.** Adds belong at planned discount/equilibrium with confirmation, not because cost basis hurts.
5. **Use historical 50% cycle dumps as humility**, not as a calendar bottom timer.
6. **Replace hopium sentences with process sentences.** "Waiting for 15m MSS in discount" beats "it has to bounce."

## Examples

**Situation:** Bags down 60%. User wants to 5x leverage double down.

**Application:** Hopium check. Down 60% can still lose most of what remains. No structure shift, no add. If anything, de-lever.

**Illustrative result:** Account survives to trade the real shift later.

**Situation:** BTC rallies ~32% off a local low on declining volume after a large drawdown; timeline says bull is back.

**Application:** Compare to historical counter-rally averages, demand structure confirmation, say "no, we are not back" until evidence prints.

**Situation:** Community only posts green P&L screenshots from the bounce.

**Application:** Ask for the invalidation and the HTF structure. Screenshots are not MSS.

## Anti-Patterns (tactical)

**Don't:** Comfort people with "it can't go lower."

**Why:** It can. That sentence is how hopium spreads.

**Don't:** Confuse anti-hopium with never buying.

**Why:** Buying discount after confirmation is process. Buying pain is hopium.

**Don't:** Use cycle averages as exact targets.

**Why:** Averages describe history. Live structure decides the trade.
