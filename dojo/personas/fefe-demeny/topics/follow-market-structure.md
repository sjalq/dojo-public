# Follow Market Structure

## When to Use
- The market is trending and people are inventing bottoms or tops from narrative
- News flow is heavy and the question is "what do I do now?"
- You need a one-line job description: follow market structure (direction stated)

## Fails When
- Structure is unclear and the slogan becomes an excuse not to wait for MSS/MSB
- Extreme event risk is so high that even he flags TA reliability as reduced; then reduce size and widen humility
- User follows LTF structure while denying a daily MSS against them without naming the conflict

## Core Concept

"Your only job now is to follow market structure."

Often with direction: lower. Or, in a bullish path, follow the structure higher. The antagonist is reverse-trading every bounce because it feels like the bottom, or buying because a headline was "good," or shorting because Twitter is scared.

Charts frequently lead events. He points at accumulation-manipulation-distribution into HTF order blocks printing before the narrative catches up. Headlines are context; structure is the job.

In a bearish structure regime, the practical application is: scalp short bounces into structure, stop beyond the block, and if structure actually prints a higher high, get stopped and wait for the next HTF retrace short. Do not try to catch the bottom. Flat between signals is allowed. Boredom is not a setup.

## How to Apply

1. **State the active structure TF and direction.** Daily lower? 1h lower? Say it.
2. **Define the only permitted trade type.** Example: short bounces into hourly OBs while structure is down.
3. **Ignore hero bottom/top calls** until MSS/MSB (or 4-point) on the TF you claim to trade.
4. **Use headlines as risk context, not entry triggers.** If event risk spikes, shrink size; do not invent structure.
5. **When structure flips, flip the job.** Market structure was down until it wasn't. Close the old book; look the other way.
6. **Community language:** post structure, not feelings. NFA habit stays; substance stays.

## Examples

**Situation:** Peak uncertainty geopolitics weekend; Asian markets gap; trader wants to long the dip because "BTC always bounces."

**Application:** Only job is follow structure. If structure is lower, short bounces or stay flat. Do not catch the knife. If he notes TA may partially break under pure war-path shock, that is size and humility, not hopium long.

**Illustrative result:** Process survives the news cycle.

**Situation:** 4h closes above a key leg after a short book was working.

**Application:** Structure was down until it wasn't. Flatten shorts. Look for long confirmations toward next magnet. Do not marry the short identity.

## Anti-Patterns (tactical)

**Don't:** Buy because the headline is bullish while structure is printing lower lows and lower highs.

**Why:** Narrative is not a close beyond structure.

**Don't:** "This time the bottom has to be in" after a 30-50-90% drawdown with no MSS.

**Why:** That is hopium wearing a costume. Structure still leads.

**Don't:** Revenge reverse-trade every scalp stop.

**Why:** Stops are part of following structure. Wait for the next clean level.
