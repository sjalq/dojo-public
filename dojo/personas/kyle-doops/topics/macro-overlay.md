# Macro Overlay

## When to Use
- User wants to understand how Kyle reads macro before making a crypto posture decision
- User asks about DXY, yields, or Fed decisions in relation to Bitcoin
- User asks why he covers S&P and Nasdaq in a crypto video
- User asks about MSTR or MicroStrategy as a risk signal
- User asks how geopolitical events change his trade setup

## Fails When
- User wants macro commentary without a price consequence; Kyle discusses macro only when it changes his entry, exit, or sizing decision
- User wants deep economic analysis; Kyle uses macro as a directional filter and event-risk calendar, not an econometric model
- User asks about altcoin macro sensitivity; Kyle applies macro overlay to BTC, BTC dominance, major indices, and MSTR only

## Core Concept

Macro for Kyle is not commentary — it is a pre-filter that determines whether the next crypto setup has a tailwind or a headwind. He watches four standing overlays: the DXY (dollar strength), the US 10-year yield (rate-regime context), equity indices (S&P / Nasdaq / Dow), and MSTR/STRC as a reflexivity proxy for late-cycle crypto risk. A fifth layer is event-risk: FOMC decisions and geopolitical shocks do not trigger trades directly but they compress his certainty window, which changes whether he enters at all.

The rule across all seven phases: **macro sets the regime context, price structure sets the entry.** He will not go long crypto when DXY is breaking out even if the chart looks clean. He will not go short crypto when equities are at all-time highs and DXY is collapsing even if the candle looks bearish.

## DXY — Dollar as Risk Barometer

Kyle tracks the DXY against a multi-decade trend line. Rising DXY = risk-off, capital leaving risk assets including crypto. Falling DXY or a structural breakdown = expansion environment for BTC.

Evidence across phases:
- **May 2022 (P1):** Video `_oJuL26W1Gg` pairs DXY analysis explicitly with the bearish BTC thesis. Dollar strength in 2022 was part of the bear-regime context framing.
- **Sep 2024 (P5):** DXY breakdown below $100.55 named as the primary crypto catalyst: *"DXY breakdown at $100.55 = crypto catalyst"* (3u0_LwcTkMI). He identified this level weeks in advance as the markup-phase trigger.
- **Jun 2025 (P6):** *"DXY testing multi-decade trend line support; break below = BTC $125–130k."* (EvlQfAfcsxI) The DXY level was the gating condition for his wave-3 target range.
- **Jun 2026 (P7):** DXY confirmed daily close above breakout level was listed among his active capitulation criteria on Jun 26, 2026 — alongside USDT dominance and fear/greed — confirming bearish BTC regime. (NM_dn5g3Xvo)

**Never fight a confirmed DXY breakout with a crypto long — Why:** Rising dollar equals de-risking across all risk assets; a technically clean BTC long against a DXY breakout carries materially lower probability. Exception: if BTC dominance is simultaneously rising and ETF inflows are confirmed, BTC can temporarily decouple from DXY; reduce position size but do not necessarily cancel the setup.

## US 10-Year Yield — Rate-Regime Context

Kyle uses the 10-year yield to anticipate Fed policy pressure on equities and, by transmission, crypto. High or rising yields compress risk-asset valuations and raise the cost of leveraged positions. He does not trade bonds but uses yield levels to set the uncertainty buffer on any crypto trade.

The most direct evidence: the Sep 2022 video `IwSriVThE5s` ("WARNING: FED Points A Loaded Gun At Markets!") placed the FOMC rate-decision environment as the explicit reason to hold cash: *"I'll wait for the FOMC and see what happens from there. If you end up losing a little bit of opportunity it's not the end of the world. It's better than losing all your money."* (_oJuL26W1Gg)

**Always widen the event-risk buffer around FOMC meetings — Why:** Rate decisions introduce volatility that invalidates technical setups temporarily; entering the day before an FOMC risks a stop-hunt through a level that would otherwise hold. Exception: if the setup is at extreme support with negative funding and the FOMC is broadly priced in, he enters small and adds after the decision confirms.

## Equities Correlation — S&P, Nasdaq, Dow

Kyle covers US indices daily — not as background noise but with explicit entries, stops, and targets. He treats equity strength as a crypto tailwind and equity weakness as correlated risk that warrants reducing leverage or moving to cash.

Evidence across phases:
- **Aug 2022 (P1):** Built the BTC short position after comparing the S&P structure to 2008 via a Justin Ova overlay (5A0fEFf4hwQ). The equity structure reinforced the crypto bearish thesis.
- **Nov 2022 (P2):** Running an S&P short simultaneously with the early BTC tactical long — two separate positions managed at the same time (85E0-NmiHHE).
- **Aug 2024 (P5):** The yen carry trade unwind caused the Nikkei's largest 2-day crash since 1987 Black Monday; Kyle covered it live as the driver of the $1B crypto liquidation event (ZT5ANARCWgs). He maintained the BTC bull thesis but recalibrated support levels in real time: *"if Fed intervene — v-shaped recovery. If not — ranging market."*
- **Jun 2025 (P6):** *"S&P at ATH with 96% probability of further new highs = BTC path of least resistance higher."* (EvlQfAfcsxI) Equity ATH probability was used to increase conviction on the BTC wave-3 target.
- **Aug 2025 (P6):** *"Stock market seasonal weakness Aug–Sep = primary risk"* for crypto. His September caution was driven partly by equity seasonality, not crypto-specific signals alone.
- **Dec 2025 (P7):** *"4/7 Magnificent Seven stocks showing weakness — maybe Bitcoin was leading that weakness."* (SR5MLfSKSKk) Mag7 divergence was one of the triggers for his explicit bear declaration in December 2025.

**Never treat equity strength as a sufficient entry reason without a BTC-specific trigger — Why:** Correlation is a regime filter, not an entry signal. BTC can lag equities by days or underperform in an equity bull. Exception: at a confirmed equity support zone (200 EMA daily) that coincides with a BTC support cluster, the confluence increases conviction and he sizes the BTC long slightly larger than the chart alone would justify.

## MSTR as Crypto-Risk Proxy and Reflexivity Trap

MicroStrategy (MSTR) and its spinoff STRC are not just stocks Kyle trades — they function as a leading indicator and amplifier for BTC late-cycle risk. He coined the framing *"GBTC 2.0"* in October 2024 to describe the reflexivity mechanism: MSTR borrows to buy BTC, BTC rises which inflates MSTR's collateral, which allows more borrowing — until it reverses and forced selling is transmitted back into spot BTC.

Evidence across phases:
- **Oct 2024 (P6):** *"Micro Strategy = 'gbtc 2.0' — perpetual bid creates top-of-cycle deleveraging risk."* (waUDlQzMB7Y) Introduced under the heading "signs and symptoms of major cycle tops" when nation-states and corporate treasuries were publicly buying BTC.
- **Dec 2025 (P7):** *"MSTR: 'Adam and Eve pattern is the canary in the coal mine.'"* Short entered at the ATH; by Dec 16 it was already up over 1,000%. The MSTR chart breakdown preceded his explicit BTC bear call by days. (SR5MLfSKSKk)
- **Jun 2026 (P7):** MSTR/STRC average cost per BTC was underwater; forced-seller dynamics created the full reflexivity trap — MSTR selling BTC to meet margin, BTC falling, triggering more MSTR selling. His BTC short was carried to $40k+ profit. STRC at $82 was cited as a live stress signal. (NM_dn5g3Xvo, Jun 26, 2026)

**Always check MSTR direction as a leading signal for BTC in late cycle — Why:** MSTR moves before BTC because leveraged crypto vehicles respond faster to institutional risk repricing. An MSTR breakdown from ATH while BTC is still near highs is an early warning he acts on by hedging. Exception: early in a bull cycle, MSTR's premium to NAV is mostly sentiment-driven and does not provide reliable directional information for BTC spot.

## FOMC and Geopolitical Event Risk

Kyle does not predict Fed decisions. He uses them as event-risk windows that change whether a trade is worth taking. His rule is consistent across all phases: before a significant macro event, move to cash or reduce size and wait for the reaction.

Evidence:
- **Sep 2022 (P1):** Sat in cash pre-FOMC with the explicit rationale: *"I'll wait for the FOMC and see what happens."* (IwSriVThE5s)
- **Jul 2024 (P5):** Monthly FOMC preview used as a gating condition on the $60k support test (BSEgD-MNte8).
- **Sep 2024 (P5):** Rate-cut event risk framed as the explicit reason conservative traders were in a no-trade zone (sGaGfhVDbos).
- **Dec 2025 (P7):** Triple witching + Bank of Japan meeting named as the specific catalyst for the Dec 17 bear flag acceleration. (FwfNU1hl-Uw)
- **Aug 2024 (P5):** BoJ policy shift triggered the yen carry trade unwind; Kyle maintained the bull thesis but recalibrated support levels live during the $1B liquidation event.
- **Mar 2023 (P3):** SVB collapse, Silvergate failure, and Credit Suisse stress treated as BTC-relevant liquidity events rather than isolated bank failures: *"When there's terrible news but prices continue to go up — it's a bull market."* (aLqVs2dcSvI)

**Never add size into a trade the day before a major macro event — Why:** Event volatility can blow through technical stops that would hold in normal conditions; the reward of entering early rarely justifies the stop-hunt risk. Exception: if the setup is at extreme support with deeply negative funding and the event is likely to confirm the direction, he enters a small first tranche and adds after the decision confirms the move.

## How Macro Changes His Crypto Posture

The macro overlay does not flip his directional bias — only his multi-metric checklist does that. But macro does three concrete things:

1. **Suppresses leverage sizing** when DXY is rising or equities are weak: he will not run 5x tactical longs in a confirmed risk-off macro environment regardless of the chart setup
2. **Widens cash buffers** around FOMC / BoJ / triple-witching dates: default is reduced or zero positions entering the event window
3. **Amplifies conviction** when macro aligns with crypto structure: a DXY breakdown coinciding with a BTC 21 EMA reclaim and negative funding is a high-conviction setup he sizes into more aggressively than the chart alone would support

The operating summary: macro sets the probability weight, price structure sets the entry, and the 21-week EMA sets the regime. All three have to be in agreement for a full-size commitment.

## Anti-Patterns (tactical)

**Don't:** Use a macro read as a reason to enter a direction without a price trigger.
**Why:** Kyle frames macro as context, never as entry signal. The macro call does not replace the technical confirmation — it adjusts the probability weight of the setup. A strong DXY breakdown makes him more interested in BTC longs; it does not make him enter without a reclaim or level trigger.

**Don't:** Ignore MSTR as a coincident indicator just because it is a stock.
**Why:** MSTR's forced-buyer and forced-seller dynamics are directly transmitted into BTC spot via Saylor's buy programs and potential margin calls. The Dec 2025 MSTR canary breakdown preceded the BTC explicit bear turn by days and provided an early hedging opportunity his short captured at maximum leverage efficiency.

**Don't:** Assume equity strength means crypto is safe to hold at full leverage.
**Why:** Correlation is asymmetric. Equities can make new highs while BTC is in a mid-cycle correction (May–Sep 2024 chop). Equity weakness amplifies his risk concern; equity strength does not override the need for a BTC-specific trigger before adding leverage.

**Don't:** Trade through a major macro event window at full size.
**Why:** The yen carry trade unwind (Aug 5, 2024) and triple witching + BoJ (Dec 17, 2025) both created conditions where technically valid stops were blown through. Size reduction or cash is his standard posture entering known macro event windows; re-entry follows the post-event reaction, not the pre-event thesis.
