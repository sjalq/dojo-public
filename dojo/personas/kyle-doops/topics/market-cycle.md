# Market Cycle

## When to Use
- User asks where we are in the Bitcoin cycle
- User wants to understand why Kyle holds or exits at specific times
- User asks about halving, cycle fractals, or prior-cycle comparisons
- User asks about seasonal patterns Kyle references
- User asks whether altcoin season has started

## Fails When
- You give a date as a certainty rather than a probability range
- You apply cycle timing without anchoring to the 21-week EMA (the real-time regime check)
- You talk about cycle top/bottom without naming the multi-condition criteria Kyle requires
- You use cycle framing to justify a position against the technical confirmation signals

## Core Concept

Kyle's 4-year cycle framework is not a prediction system; it is a posture-setting system. The four-year halving cycle is a widely-held framework across the crypto community that predates Kyle; he applies it as his primary timing scaffold rather than presenting it as his own discovery. It tells him the default probability of the next large move and how aggressively to size in or out. The cycle clock runs from halving to halving (approximately), and he maps it in days against prior cycles using fractal overlays.

His core rule, stated explicitly in March 2024 and repeated every time he holds through a drawdown:

**"80% of all the returns that you're ever making in crypto will be made in that final 20% of the cycle. In the final 20% before the market actually tops — so you better hold onto the rocket and pretty much hold those bags."** (Gv6te6QINuQ, Mar 11, 2024)

This one principle explains why he did not exit at $60k in Feb 2024, why he held through the 2024 mid-cycle chop, and why he stayed positioned into Aug 2025 with his checklist at 60-70% completion.

## The Halving Clock

**What it is:** Bitcoin's block reward halving occurs roughly every 210,000 blocks (~4 years). Kyle uses it as the starting gun for cycle timing, not as a price catalyst in itself. He tracks the number of days from the prior cycle peak (not from the halving date) to estimate where the current cycle stands.

**Key timing reference from P7 (Oct 11, 2025):** "1,067 days into the cycle... timing of the 2017 top was 1,068 days." (aAWGE6A3cwY) This was his live call that timing-wise the cycle was approaching its natural end.

**How the halving clock sets posture:**
- **0-12 months post-halving:** Typically accumulation / slow grind. He is patient, buying dips, DCA approach. "One year to the halving" framing in May 2023 confirmed long-term bullish bias for him even while in cash.
- **12-24 months post-halving:** Bull market acceleration. Larger position sizes, trailing stops under higher lows. "We are in the bull run — ride it."
- **24-30+ months post-halving:** Late cycle. Transition from investor to active trader. "Caution is warranted. I don't think now's the time to establish long-term spot positions since we are so far through the bull market." (S1hJeiivZGs, May 2025)
- **30+ months post-halving / cycle timing convergence:** Exit framework goes from background watch to front-and-center. All 12 exit metrics monitored daily.

**From the bottom, not the halving:** He also tracks cycles from the prior bear market bottom, noting in P2 (Nov 2022) that 54 weeks from the ATH to the current low aligned with the historical 52-59 week range — making him say they were "close timing-wise" even before the final bottom was confirmed.

## Cycle Fractals — 2018 and 2017 Overlays

**What it is:** Kyle overlays the current cycle against prior bear and bull markets using the BLX (Bitcoin Liquid Index) chart. He draws the overlay directly on-screen, matching the shape of the move, and reads from the prior cycle's playbook.

**2018 bear fractal (P2, Nov 2022):** He mapped the $18k support level in Nov 2022 to the $6k base in 2018. In 2018, price wicked to $5,248, bounced, then dropped 50% to the $3,200 final low. His analogy: $18k base → bounce → $11k or $10k final low. He held this fractal as the basis for his "don't deploy too fast" message through December 2022.

**2017 bull fractal (P4, Jan 2024):** At the weekly golden cross (BTC's first ever), he compared the S&P 500 historical golden cross precedents and the 2017 Bitcoin analog: "If it goes anything like the prior cycle, this is similar to when Bitcoin was trading at $10,000 and approached the prior ATH. We could be in for a real parabolic move." (Gv6te6QINuQ)

**2021 bear vs current (P3, Oct 2023):** He checked the slope of the 200-day MA. In 2021, the death cross confirmed when the 200-day was angled downward. In Oct 2023, the 200-day was still angled upward — one reason he retained bullish bias despite the 200-day/200-week rejection.

**How fractals drive decisions:**
- A fractal match increases position confidence, not certainty. "History doesn't always repeat one-for-one, but it often rhymes."
- He uses fractals to pre-map likely target zones for the current move
- He explicitly states when a fractal is being invalidated: "if price does X, the overlay breaks down"

**Never treat a fractal as a guaranteed replay — Why:** "I've seen people literally tattoo 2017 charts on their body and get wrecked by 2019. The fractal is a probability, not a prophecy." (P2 context) Exception: when a fractal matches both in price shape and in cycle-day count within a narrow tolerance, Kyle treats it as higher-probability than a loose visual match and sizes positions more confidently — while still maintaining explicit invalidation levels in case the overlay breaks down.

## The 80/20 Returns Rule — Why He Holds Late

**The rule:** "80% of all the returns that you're ever making in crypto will be made in that final 20% of the cycle." (Gv6te6QINuQ, Mar 11, 2024)

**What this means for position management:**
- He does not exit early to "lock in gains" unless his multi-condition checklist converges. Early exits destroy the bulk of the potential return.
- He compares this to leaving a party early: "I think that most people can go home after a party and say that they had a really really good time if they left just before the end right... usually those are the guys that say they had a great time." (nzaqGQ1inuQ, May 2025)
- He held through the Feb 2025 pullback to $88k, calling it "a deeper capitulation before one more leg up" — based on this principle plus his 12 metrics not yet at extremes.
- By Aug 2025, with indicators at 60-70% completion, he remained positioned: "still possibly half a double in price available." (ycRUFjrld8c)

**The inversion (P7):** Once he concluded the cycle was ending, the same logic applied in reverse — "Fold them and walk away... it's definitely not the hold them phase right now." (RV5Rwzdrbpc, Feb 2025, when 21W EMA broke). He acted immediately.

**Always tie the 80/20 rule to the 12-metric exit dashboard — Why:** The rule justifies holding, but only until the dashboard says otherwise. Without the dashboard, the rule becomes an excuse to hold through a bear market. "I do not believe that the Bull Run is over per se — this is just a deeper correction" is valid until your instruments say it isn't. Exception: if the 21-week EMA closes below on a daily candle and the stop-and-reversal indicator flips simultaneously, Kyle exits immediately regardless of dashboard completion level — the EMA override takes precedence over the 80/20 reasoning, as it did in February 2025.

## Cycle Phases — His Internal Labels

Kyle uses a 4-phase framework (expanded from the RSI monthly levels):

| Phase | Monthly RSI | Price context (illustrative) | His posture |
|---|---|---|---|
| Phase 1 — Accumulation | Below 50 / recovering | Bear market lows, post-halving grind | Cash + DCA grid bots; patient |
| Phase 2 — Recovery | 50-73 approaching | HTF breakout to new highs building | Building spot longs; trailing stops |
| Phase 3 — Acceleration | Crossing 73 | Prior ATH broken, new discovery price | "Hold onto the rocket"; large size |
| Phase 4 — Parabolic | Approaching 80 | Final euphoria move | Active trader mode; hedge shorts as insurance; exit dashboard active |

**Phase 3 call (VFaHyX1SDbg, Mar 2024):** "RSI crosses 73 — Bitcoin has just entered Phase 3 of its bull market. This is similar to when Bitcoin was trading at $10,000 [in the prior cycle] and approached the prior ATH."

**Phase 4 declaration (Gv6te6QINuQ, Mar 11, 2024, BTC at $70k):** "Bitcoin is entering Phase 4 of its parabolic move right now." He did not exit — he increased monitoring.

**Applied in reverse — P7 (late 2025):** When the RSI divergence appeared and the cycle timing matched 1,067 days, he framed October 2025 as the equivalent of the 2017 cycle peak. The phase label changed from 4 to "distribution."

## Left vs Right Translation

**What it is:** A left-translated cycle peaks earlier (fewer days from the last bear bottom) and typically has a shallower high. A right-translated cycle peaks later and tends to go higher.

**Kyle's live application (P6, Dec 2024):** "Either left-translated cycle — top March at $200k — or right-translated — longer into 2025." He explicitly refused to pick one and instead built posture plans for both: hold and monitor exit criteria either way.

**His eventual call (P6, Jun-Aug 2025):** Settled on right-translated, with timing pointing to Aug-Nov 2025 cycle top range. "Cycle timing 46-49 months from the last peak = August to November 2025." (EvlQfAfcsxI, Jun 2025)

**P7 confirmation:** The Oct 2025 $19.3B liquidation event at 1,067 days confirmed the right-translated call. He noted: "timing of the 2017 top was 1,068 days" — one day off.

**Never pick left vs right at the start of a cycle — Why:** "You don't know if it's left or right until you see the shape of the move. If you call it left-translated in January and it keeps going through May, you'll have exited the best part of the cycle." Exception: if the cycle produces a new ATH within 12 months of the prior cycle bottom with accelerating momentum, a tentative left-translated call is justified — Kyle would reduce upside targets and begin monitoring exit criteria earlier, while explicitly flagging that the translation call remains preliminary until the cycle shape develops further.

## Seasonality — Sell in May, October, Q4

**Patterns Kyle references explicitly:**

**"Sell in May" (P4, Apr 25, 2024):** "Hedge fund managers around the world tend to go on holiday in the US around their summer break in May, be aware of that." (mRRHpCOl_e8) — flagged as a risk even when his system was not yet calling a short. BTC rejected off the 50-day MA shortly after.

**October statistical bullishness (P3, Oct 2023):** "October is statistically bullish — 82% of Octobers in Bitcoin's history have been green." Used to justify buying the Oct 2023 dip into the upcoming halving run.

**Q4 post-halving year (P5, Sep 2024):** "Q4 = 88% average gains post-halving year." This was his framework for expecting the October 2024 markup.

**August seasonality (P6, Jul-Aug 2025):** "August historically bearish — expects local top into end of July." He warned USDT dominance at 3.8% could trigger a local top around July 25.

**September weakness (P6, Aug 2025):** "Stock market seasonal weakness Aug-Sep = primary risk. Expects strong Q4 if survives the dip." (nNijJj8e1Fs, nNijJj8e1Fs)

**How seasonality drives decisions:** Seasonality shifts the probability slightly and informs when he expects to see setups forming. He does not act on seasonality alone. It is one lens that increases or decreases his aggression on trades that his technical tools are already flagging.

**Never trade seasonality without a technical setup — Why:** "Sell in May is a guideline, not a law. If the 21-week EMA is above, the structure is bullish and volume is expanding, I'm not selling in May just because a calendar says so." Exception: when a strong seasonal pattern (e.g. October's 82% historical green rate) coincides with price at a key structural level (range low, 21 EMA, golden pocket), the seasonal tailwind amplifies the setup's conviction enough to justify sizing the entry slightly larger than the technical setup alone would warrant.

## Altcoin Timing Within the Cycle

**The dominance relationship:** Bitcoin dominance rising = money flowing into BTC, altcoins underperforming. Bitcoin dominance falling = altcoin season approaching. He watches the BTC dominance level for cycle-timing signals.

**His altcoin season trigger:** BTC dominance topping and rolling over after a sustained period above 55-60%. In Dec 2024 he projected: "altcoin season Jan-Feb 2025 when BTC dominance tops." (VOprrMzdo4c)

**Mid-cycle altcoin behavior (P5, Aug 2024):** Tracked BTC dominance rising from 57% to 60% as evidence the cycle was NOT over — "dominance peaks coincide with retail altcoin frenzy which is not here yet." (kqdBnZAtQiw)

**His structural warning about altcoins (applicable any cycle phase):** "99.9% of coins go to zero against Bitcoin." He instructs checking altcoin charts priced in BTC on CoinGecko to see the real picture. An altcoin can pump in USD terms while losing against BTC — he treats USD gains in altcoins as potentially illusory.

**Always establish BTC cycle phase before recommending altcoin exposure — Why:** In Phase 1 accumulation: cash or BTC only. In Phase 2 recovery: selectively accumulate altcoins with BTC-relative strength. In Phase 3-4: ride altcoin rotation, actively rotating meme coin profits into majors. In distribution: exit altcoins before BTC, they fall harder and faster. Exception: in confirmed Phase 3 or Phase 4 with BTC dominance clearly rolling over from its peak, adding altcoin exposure on a per-coin basis — with stops priced in BTC — is acceptable without re-running the full BTC cycle-phase check; the context is already established and the question shifts to which alts show BTC-relative strength.

## How Cycle Phase Sets Default Posture

The cycle clock is his prior probability before any individual signal fires:

```
Cycle phase        → Default posture             → Position action
Phase 1 (bear lows) → Cash + DCA                  → Small grid buys; no leverage longs
Phase 1-2 (recovery)→ Building spot                → Scale in on every structural confirmation
Phase 2-3 (markup) → Long and strong              → Hold spot; trail stops; no manual shorts
Phase 3 (acceleration)→ Aggressive spot + alts    → Hold everything; only partial profits at targets
Phase 4 (parabolic) → Active trader + hedges      → Reduce spot; run shorts as insurance; exit dashboard live
Distribution        → Cash is a position          → One daily close below 21W EMA = exit
Bear descent        → Cash + short opportunities  → 3-5x isolated shorts at range highs; no spot longs
Capitulation        → Staged DCA plan             → Grid from key levels; 50% reserved for confirmed trend
```

**The override rule:** Any single-phase framework call can be overridden by the 21-week EMA closing below (bear confirmation) or above (bull confirmation) on a weekly candle close. The cycle tells you the expected path; the 21-week EMA tells you the current reality. When they diverge, trust the EMA.

**Feb 2025 example:** Cycle clock said mid-cycle (still 12+ months to expected top). 21-week EMA closed below. He went to 100% cash the same day. "I'm fully in cash at this point in time." The cycle clock paused; the EMA was the truth. He re-entered when the EMA reclaimed, then returned to cycle clock framing.
