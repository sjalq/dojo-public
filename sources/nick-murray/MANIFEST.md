# Nick Murray - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Nick Murray persona. Murray publishes almost no interviews —
his primary voice is his own monthly newsletter (*Nick Murray Interactive*)
and his books. He has written twelve books for financial advisors plus one
for investors (*Simple Wealth, Inevitable Wealth*), and gives very few media
interviews by design.

`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Murray is the sole guest on someone else's
show, doing nearly all the talking; safe for voice/context. No
`interview-host` sources exist in this corpus — Murray has no podcast of his
own, so there is no guest-contamination risk from that direction. Newsletter
essays and book material authored directly by Murray are implicitly `type:
author` (no tag needed). Several third-party summary/quote pages are marked
in their own notes as AI-summarized or third-party — those are used for
framework-scouting and context only, never for verbatim quotation.
