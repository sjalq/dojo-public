# Magnus Heystek - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Magnus Heystek persona. Magnus Heystek is a South African investment
strategist, co-founder/director of Brenthurst Wealth Management, and a former
long-time financial journalist (business/investment editor at The Star; a 14-year
personal-finance newspaper column; 11 years hosting a phone-in finance show on
Radio 702; presenter of "Geldsake met Moneyweb" on RSG). His core, decades-long
thesis is that South African sovereign, currency, and policy risk make maximizing
tax-efficient offshore diversification the correct default for South African
investors.

Sources span three source types with different voice-safety profiles:

`type: solo` = Heystek alone (a BizNews Conference keynote address with no other
speaker) -- single-voice, safe for voice samples and direct quotation.

`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Heystek is THE interviewee on someone else's show
(usually BizNews founder Alec Hogg, but also Rob Hersov, David Ansara, Alishia
Seckam, or a named BNC/BNIC Q&A moderator) -- Heystek does nearly all the talking;
safe for voice samples and quotation.

Several YouTube videos and one article are **two-guest debates or panels**
(Heystek plus Piet Viljoen, Anthony Ginsberg, or Candice Paine, with a BizNews
host moderating). These are tagged `interview-guest:<host>` for format
consistency, but their transcripts/text are **not diarized by speaker** and
carry real risk of misattributing the other guest's words to Heystek --
treat as context only, never for direct quotation. Each such entry's `notes:`
field flags this explicitly.

Author-written material (BizNews/Moneyweb/Financial Mail/BusinessDay columns
bylined "Magnus Heystek") is the highest-fidelity voice source in this corpus
and is marked as such in `notes:` where confirmed. A large fraction of the
older Moneyweb columns sit behind a subscriber paywall (Moneyweb Insider Gold)
and could only be captured as title/teaser stubs -- flagged per-entry below.
