# Evan Czaplicki - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Evan Czaplicki persona: creator of the Elm programming language.
Articles are Evan's own writing (elm-lang.org/news essays, the official Elm
guide he authored, package design guidelines, compiler roadmap, README essays).
YouTube covers his solo conference talks (2014-2025) plus interviews where he
is the guest. Podcasts are guest appearances with published transcripts.

`type: solo` = single-voice talk, safe for voice samples and direct quotation.
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Evan is the interviewee on someone else's show;
safe for voice with care (check speaker attribution). Articles are implicitly
`type: author`.
