# Harold Evensky - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Harold Evensky persona. Solo-voice sources are his own
"Six Things" talk (recorded at his own firm) and the publisher preview of
the Preface to *The New Wealth Management* (his own prose). Interview-guest
sources (WealthTrack, Morningstar's "How to Retire," Kitces' FASuccess, Rob
Berger, Goldstein on Gelt) are one-on-one interviews where Evensky is the
guest and does nearly all of the substantive talking.

`type: solo` = single-voice, safe for voice samples and direct quotation.
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Evensky is the interviewee on someone else's
show; he does most of the talking; safe for voice.
`type: third-party-tribute` = others speaking about Evensky, not Evensky
speaking. Context/biography only, never for voice.
