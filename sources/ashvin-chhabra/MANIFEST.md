# Ashvin Chhabra - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Ashvin Chhabra persona. Chhabra is not a high-volume public
content creator — his public corpus is a 2005 academic paper, one book, a
handful of long-form guest interviews, two author-written essays, and a run
of press Q&As, most of it clustered around the 2015 book launch plus
periodic re-surfacing (2021-2025) as a family-office CIO.

`type: solo` = Chhabra alone (a lecture/talk), safe for voice samples and
direct quotation. `type: interview-guest:HOST` = he is the sole guest on
someone else's show and does nearly all the talking; safe for voice and
quotation. One YouTube entry below is a three-way panel (two guests plus a
moderator) and is flagged unsafe for direct quotation despite carrying an
`interview-guest:` tag — see its `notes:`.

## Books

- title: Beyond Markowitz: A Comprehensive Wealth Allocation Framework for Individual Investors (Ashvin B. Chhabra, 2005)
  source: https://jwm.pm-research.com/content/7/4/8
  added: 2026-07-09
  notes: Originally published in The Journal of Wealth Management, vol. 7, no. 4 (Spring 2005), pp. 8-34. Full text also freely mirrored at contemplata.it. Originated the Wealth Allocation Framework (Personal / Market / Aspirational risk buckets) — the single highest-value source in the corpus.

- title: The Aspirational Investor: Taming the Markets to Achieve Your Life's Goals (Ashvin B. Chhabra, HarperCollins, 2015)
  source: https://www.aspirationalinvestor.com/
  added: 2026-07-09
  notes: ISBN 9780062235091. Full text not part of this corpus (book, not freely republished). Chapters 1-12 popularize the Wealth Allocation Framework for a general-investor audience. Substitute primary-voice coverage of the same argument comes from the 2005 paper above and the IAS talk below.
