Corpus for the Jordan Peterson persona. Peterson's primary written corpus is
four mass-market books (Maps of Meaning, 1999; 12 Rules for Life, 2018;
Beyond Order, 2021; We Who Wrestle with God, 2024) which cannot be fetched
here — covered instead via his own solo lectures on the same material (the
2017 Maps of Meaning and Biblical Series lecture series restate the book's
architecture in his own voice) and a legitimate publisher excerpt of the
newest book. His spoken corpus splits cleanly: solo university lectures,
tour lectures, and conference keynotes (voice-safe, `type: solo`) versus
long-form interviews where he is the guest (`type: interview-guest:HOST`,
voice-safe — he does most of the talking) versus two public debates where
a co-equal peer speaks at comparable length (flagged below — treat as
mixed-voice, context-only, tagged `interview-host:NAME`).

`type: solo` = single-voice, safe for voice samples and direct quotation.
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Peterson is the interviewee; his answers are
voice-safe, the host's/interviewer's words are not.
`type: interview-host:NAME` = the canonical mixed-voice tag. Peterson does
not host either of these events; they are moderated two-person public
debates where NAME speaks at length comparable to Peterson's own, and the
tag is used (as in other dojo manifests for shared-stage events) because it
is the process's only mixed-voice class. Auto-captions are not diarized.
Context only: never quote, never use for voice samples, never extract a
framework whose only source is one of these two files.
Articles authored by Peterson are implicitly `type: author`.
