# Jean Brunel - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Jean Brunel persona. Jean L.P. Brunel, CFA, is the former Chief
Investment Officer of J.P. Morgan's Global Private Bank (1990–2001), founder of
Brunel Associates (2001), and Editor-in-Chief of *The Journal of Wealth
Management* since its 1998 founding. He is the originator of goals-based wealth
management — the framework the CFA program treats as the preferred approach to
private-client portfolio construction at Level III.

The corpus is smaller than a mass-media persona's because Brunel is a B2B
practitioner and journal editor, not a public-facing content creator — his
primary output is peer-reviewed journal writing and CFA Institute conference
material, most of which sits behind a subscription paywall. The crown-jewel
sources are a 2012 CFA Institute conference paper (public PDF) in which
Brunel is the sole author and sole respondent in the accompanying Q&A; the
complete verbatim text of Chapter 1 of his 2015 book, obtained via the
publisher's own excerpt PDF; and a ~2,990-word CFA Institute "Take 15" audio
interview, recovered and transcribed in a second research pass. Together
these give three distinct first-person registers — written manifesto/
diagnostic/tactical prose, narrative/reflective book prose, and spoken
interview Q&A.

`type: solo` = Brunel is the only voice (author pieces, solo conference talks/Q&A).
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Brunel is the interviewee on someone else's show;
he does most of the talking. Safe for voice sampling. No `interview-host`
sources exist for Brunel — he does not host his own podcast.
