# John Carmack - Source Manifest

Corpus for the John Carmack persona. Carmack's spoken corpus is unusually
voice-safe: his signature format is the solo, unscripted, multi-hour keynote
(QuakeCon 2002-2013, Oculus/Facebook/Meta Connect 2014-2022, Upper Bound 2025).
His written corpus is his own: .plan development diaries (1996-2010), technical
essays, Armadillo Aerospace updates, and long-form Facebook posts. YouTube
transcripts are auto-captions except where noted.

`type: solo` = single-voice, safe for voice samples and direct quotation.
Keynote Q&A segments contain audience questions - Carmack's answers are
quotable, audience prompts are not.
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Carmack is the interviewee; his answers are
voice-safe, the host's words are not.
Articles authored by Carmack are implicitly `type: author`.
