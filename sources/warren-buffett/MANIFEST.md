# Warren Buffett - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Warren Buffett persona. The single best voice source is
Buffett's own prose: Berkshire Hathaway shareholder letters (1977-2024,
freely published at berkshirehathaway.com), his farewell/retirement message
(Nov 2025), his 1984 essay/talk "The Superinvestors of Graham-and-Doddsville",
his 2008 NYT op-ed "Buy American. I Am.", and his 2010 "My Philanthropic
Pledge" letter. All shareholder letters were Buffett's own unedited work
through the 2024 letter (published Feb 2025) -- his 60th and final letter as
CEO/author. Greg Abel wrote the first CEO letter for FY2025 (published
2026-03); Abel's letters are NOT part of this persona's voice corpus.

Spoken material is CNBC/Becky Quick sit-down interviews spanning 2013-2026
(annual "Ask Warren" sessions plus the two 2026 interviews given after
Buffett stepped down as CEO), plus one official Berkshire annual-meeting Q&A
video that is explicitly MIXED VOICE (Buffett + Greg Abel + Ajit Jain) and
tagged accordingly.

`type: author` (implicit, no tag needed) = Buffett's own written letters/essays/op-eds.
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Buffett is the interviewee; he does nearly all
the talking. Safe for voice samples and direct quotation.
`type: interview-host:GUEST` = mixed voice (Buffett shares the stage with
others answering in rotation). Context and framework-mining only -- never
quote without verifying the speaker line-by-line.

## Skipped

- title: The Essays of Warren Buffett - Lessons for Corporate America (Lawrence Cunningham, ed.)
  source: book, no legally obtainable full text
  reason: Compiled/edited anthology of Buffett's own shareholder letters, curated by a third party (Cunningham) and organized thematically. No piracy attempted. The underlying primary-source material (the original letters, 1977-2024) is already in this corpus directly from berkshirehathaway.com at higher fidelity and completeness than the anthology's excerpts. Not worth a separate fetch.
  date: 2026-07-09

- title: The Snowball - Warren Buffett and the Business of Life (Alice Schroeder, 2008 biography)
  source: book, no legally obtainable full text
  reason: Biography by a third party, not Buffett's own writing. Per corpus-prioritization rules, biographies are lowest priority and should only be mined for direct Buffett quotes, not paraphrase. Given strong direct-voice coverage already in this corpus (20 own-written pieces + 8 solo/interview-guest transcripts), skipped rather than sought out for isolated quotes.
  date: 2026-07-09

- title: Warren Buffett Talks to MBA Students (compiled talks, various editors)
  source: book, no legally obtainable full text
  reason: Third-party compilation of talks; the corpus already includes a direct primary-source MBA/student talk (Florida University, video_id 2MHIcabnjrA) and multiple long-form interviews covering the same ground in Buffett's own words.
  date: 2026-07-09

- title: Berkshire Hathaway Annual Meeting Q&A archive (CNBC Warren Buffett Archive), pre-2024 years and Charlie Munger-era meetings generally
  source: https://buffett.cnbc.com/annual-meetings/
  reason: Every annual meeting from 1994-2023 pairs Buffett with Charlie Munger, who answers a large share of questions and often finishes or reframes Buffett's answers. Munger is already a separate dojo persona; mixed-voice risk is high and auto-captions are not diarized. One post-Munger (2024) meeting video was fetched instead (type: interview-host:Greg Abel & Ajit Jain) for framework context. Pre-2024 meeting Q&A was deliberately not fetched to avoid Munger-voice contamination; if the intake author needs more annual-meeting framework material, prefer 2024-2026 meetings only.
  date: 2026-07-09

- title: Greg Abel 2026 first CEO shareholder letter (FY2025)

  reason: Written by Greg Abel, not Warren Buffett. Buffett is Chairman, not the letter's author, as of this letter. Out of scope for Buffett's voice corpus; relevant only as latest-work/succession context, which is covered by the Nov 2025 farewell letter and the 2026 Becky Quick interviews instead.
  date: 2026-07-09
