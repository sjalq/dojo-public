# Tony Hinchcliffe - Source Manifest

Corpus for the Tony Hinchcliffe persona. Hinchcliffe has written almost no long-form
prose of his own - his corpus is overwhelmingly spoken: long-form guest interviews
(Joe Rogan Experience, Theo Von), his own show (Kill Tony), stand-up specials, roast
sets, and press quotes from print/digital interviews given as himself.

`type: solo` = single-voice, safe for voice samples and direct quotation.
`type: interview-guest:HOST` = Hinchcliffe is the interviewee on someone else's show;
his answers are voice-safe, the host's words are not. YouTube auto-captions are NOT
diarized - attribute quotes with care.
`type: interview-host:GUEST` = Hinchcliffe hosts (Kill Tony); MIXED VOICE - context
only, never direct quotation. No Kill Tony episode transcripts were acquired in this
pass; see Skipped.
Articles/press interviews: direct quotes attributed to Hinchcliffe by name are
voice-safe for that quote only; surrounding journalist narration is not.
Quotes used in the persona were located with speaker-labelled transcripts made
from the interview audio and cross-checked against the original captions.
